Undergrid Networks

Operational Reality for Critical Infrastructure

Visit Undergrid Networks

Undergrid Networks develops technology and strategic solutions for organizations responsible for essential infrastructure. Its flagship platform, UndergridOS, is designed to sit above and across existing operational and enterprise systems, not replace them.

UndergridOS connects and reconciles information from systems such as GIS, SCADA, asset management, work management, project controls, sensors, enterprise platforms, environmental sources, and external data. It creates a shared operating picture that helps organizations understand current conditions, historical context, emerging risk, competing priorities, and potential consequences.

Problem

Essential infrastructure organizations often have the data they need, but it lives across disconnected systems and teams.

Approach

UndergridOS reconciles asset, operational, project, financial, regulatory, environmental, and organizational context into one operating picture.

Result

Leaders can see what is happening, understand how conclusions were reached, and make decisions with clearer accountability.
