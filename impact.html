Impact

Technology should strengthen the systems and people expected to carry the future.

Marilyn's impact work centers on infrastructure resilience, better-supported decisions, broader participation in modernization, and practical support for founders and communities.

Commitments

Areas of commitment.

The through line is practical change: stronger systems, clearer decisions, wider access, and more resilient communities.

Critical-Infrastructure Resilience

Advancing tools and partnerships that help essential systems anticipate stress, coordinate action, preserve knowledge, and recover more effectively.

Inclusive Economic Participation

Expanding pathways for women, veterans, minority founders, small businesses, and historically underrepresented institutions.

Responsible Technology

Promoting systems in which evidence is traceable, AI-supported reasoning is governed, human authority is clear, and operational consequences remain visible.

Community-Centered Decision-Making

Encouraging leaders to consider public, environmental, and community effects alongside assets, cost, and performance.

Entrepreneurial Resilience

Sharing lessons of reinvention so founders and leaders can respond to disruption with discipline, creativity, and purpose.

Leadership

Board, advisory, mentorship, and community leadership.

Board and Corporate Governance

Founder and CEO of Undergrid Networks and Mahjee; principal managing partner of Maroti Global; advisory board roles with Nomad Platforms, AdaVerse, and Wingu Technology; former board member of Technology Association of Georgia.

Entrepreneurial Mentorship

Board member and veteran entrepreneur mentor with DAV Patriot Boot Camp; lead mentor at The Farm, Comcast NBCUniversal; former CEO in Residence at Launchpad2X; mentor for PitcHER and Career Girls Artificial Intelligence Summer Camp; former advisory board member for Girls Who Code.

Accolades and Awards

Best in Show and First Place at the Minority Business Development Agency Manufacturing Summit; Venture Atlanta presenting company; Blockchain in Healthcare speaker with Patientory.

Professional Communities

Participant in veteran entrepreneurship, supplier-diversity, minority-business development, and women-owned business communities, including NMSDC/GMSDC, NaVOBO, WBENC, and SBA woman-owned, veteran-owned, and service-disabled veteran-owned small business ecosystems.
