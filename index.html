Opening Statement

Critical decisions need better evidence than fragmented dashboards can provide.

Energy, water, transportation, communications, facilities, public services, and the built environment are increasingly connected. Many organizations still make high-stakes decisions from isolated tools, incomplete histories, and competing versions of the facts.

Through Undergrid Networks and UndergridOS, Marilyn is advancing Operational Reality: a practical view of what is happening across assets, operations, projects, risks, economics, regulatory commitments, communities, and external conditions.

The work is technical, but it is also a leadership problem. Leaders need evidence they can trust, context they can preserve, and decisions they can explain.
